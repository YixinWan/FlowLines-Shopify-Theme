# Flowlines website design contract
## Brand overview
Flowlines is an early-stage drawing-projector and guided-creation brand for curious beginners and creative hobbyists, with a premium yet approachable voice. The website's primary goal is waitlist registration, not immediate checkout.
## Design thesis
A calm editorial gallery for a clever drawing instrument: the product and the art it unlocks occupy the spotlight.
## Colors
- Canvas #FFFCF7, warm off-white; site surface.
- Ink #202326, strong type and contrast.
- Coral #D97757, CTA accent.
- Midnight #1E3446, cinematic media backgrounds.
- Mist #F3F1ED, supporting card surfaces.
## Typography
- Display: theme primary sans family, semibold/bold 600-700, 44–72px desktop, 34–44px mobile, 1.05–1.12 line height, -0.025em tracking; fallback Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif.
- Body: theme body sans family, 400–500, 16–19px, line height 1.5–1.65; fallback Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif.
## Spacing and composition
- Media-led full-width hero; centered 1280px content rail; 48px horizontal desktop gutters, 20px mobile.
- Section spacing 88–120px desktop and 56–72px mobile.
- Image cards radius 16px; primary CTA pill radius 999px. Minimal shadows, ample whitespace.
## Components
- Header: warm white, logo left and compact navigation center.
- Hero: full bleed product art, short copy, one clear waitlist CTA.
- Proof strip: factual product truths only, three evenly spaced short points.
- Feature cards: 2-up immersive photography with caption below, stacked on mobile.
- Spotlight: cinematic wide panel with subtle gradient and a short statement.
- Process: existing 3-step guide.
- Gallery: authentic artwork examples.
- FAQ: accordion with concise truthful answers.
- Waitlist: high-contrast soft-coral registration block.
- Footer: clear company and contact information, simple utility links.
- Buttons: solid coral with white for primary, dark ink text link for secondary.
- Form controls: white surface, visible label and focus indication.
## Do and don't
- Do borrow Homerunpet's large modular image-led layout and generous rhythm.
- Do keep the genuine Flowlines product visuals and editable merchant settings.
- Don't copy Homerunpet imagery, badges, text, product categories or sales claims.
- Don't invent customer counts, awards, testing endorsements or launch dates.
- Don't imply purchasing is open while the site is waitlist-first.
## Motion philosophy
Optional subtle fades or hover lifts only; no auto-playing disruptive animation. Honor reduced-motion preferences.
